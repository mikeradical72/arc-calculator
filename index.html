Assumptions

  • Before sequestration unless you tick the box. Simplified: no payment limits, AGI test, or ARC-IC rules for crops with no history; FSA's own rounding can move totals by a few dollars.
  • ARC-CO: each crop's guarantee is 90% of county benchmark revenue (county benchmark yield × benchmark price). It pays the shortfall below that, capped at 12% of benchmark revenue, on 85% of that crop's base acres.
  • ARC-IC: each crop's benchmark revenue is your farm's ARC-IC benchmark yield (2020–24 FSA history, high and low years dropped; not crop-insurance APH) × benchmark price, blended by planted acres. It pays the shortfall below 90%, capped at 12%, on 65% of the farm's total base acres. Electing ARC-IC covers every crop's base on that FSA farm.
  • PLC: the rate is the effective reference price minus the higher of the MYA price or the loan rate. It pays that rate × your PLC yield on 85% of base. PLC yields here are FSA county averages; use the ones on your FSA-156EZ.
  • Prices are the national marketing-year average (MYA), not local cash. 2026 county yields aren't published yet; FSA posts them in fall 2027, and payments come after Oct. 1, 2027.
  • This is an estimate, not a USDA determination. Check with your county FSA office before you elect.
